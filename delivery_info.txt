Highlight:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We are particularly proud of our Hemispheric Screen Space Ambient Occlusion (SSAO+) implementation. We successfully integrated the TBN matrix computation directly in the fragment shader to align the sampling hemisphere with the surface normal. This completely eliminated the incorrect self-occlusion artifacts of standard SSAO, producing clean and highly realistic contact shadows that greatly improved the overall visual quality of our scene.
